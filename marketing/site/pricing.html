Everything is included

There is no Pro tier and nothing is held back. The list below is not a plan — it is the app.

  • SSH and Mosh — Mosh sessions survive Wi-Fi/5G handoffs and heavy packet loss.
  • Native tmux and herdr — real sessions, windows and panes, not a TUI in a box.
  • Agent status on the lock screen — Allow, Deny and quick replies without unlocking.
  • Isolated tasks on a git worktree — start one from the phone, in one sheet.
  • Keyboard, themes, icon and accent — a shortcut bar you build from a key library.
  • No account and no servers of ours — nothing to sign up for, nothing collected.

The details people actually ask about

Refunds

Handled by Apple

Purchases and refunds run entirely through the App Store. We never see a card number, and we cannot process a refund ourselves — Apple's request form is the route, and it is a generous one.

Devices

iPhone and iPad, one purchase

A universal app: buy it once and it installs on both, on every device signed into the same Apple Account.

Updates

Included, not a new SKU

Updates to v1 are free. If a future major version is ever charged for separately, it will be a separate app on the store, not a subscription bolted onto this one.

Trial

There isn't one

A paid-up-front app cannot offer a trial on the App Store. That is why the documentation and the comparison are blunt about the limits — you should be able to tell before paying.

Regions

Most of the world at launch

Available across the App Store's regions including the EU, with local pricing converted by Apple. Mainland China is not in the first release — it requires an MIIT filing we do not yet hold.

Business

Why it is this cheap

One developer, no servers to run, no support desk to staff. The costs that force other terminals onto a subscription are costs this app does not have.
